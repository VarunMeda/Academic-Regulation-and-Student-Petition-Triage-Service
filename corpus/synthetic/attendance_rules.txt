SYNTHETIC TEST CORPUS - NOT AN OFFICIAL UNIVERSITY REGULATION

Clause 4.1 Minimum Attendance. A student shall ordinarily have attendance of at least 75 percent in a course to be eligible for the end-semester examination. Attendance below the prescribed minimum is not eligible under the base rule.

Clause 7.3 Medical Condonation. Where attendance is below the minimum but not below 65 percent, a shortfall may be condoned on documented medical grounds. The examination committee retains discretion to approve or refuse condonation after reviewing the medical evidence.
