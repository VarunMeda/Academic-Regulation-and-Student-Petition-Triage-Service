# Corpus provenance

The checked-in corpus is `corpus/synthetic/attendance_rules.txt`. It is explicitly synthetic and exists only for deterministic development and evaluation. It is not issued by, reviewed by, or attributable to a university.

## Official corpus procedure

Before institutional use, obtain complete academic regulations and examination bye-laws for one university and admission batch from its official publication site. Record source URL, retrieval date, document title, version/batch, checksum, page count, extraction/OCR method and responsible verifier in a source manifest. Run ingestion, inspect every clause used by evaluation cases, and update expected citations only after manual confirmation. Conflicting versions must remain separately identified and cause review rather than silent replacement.
